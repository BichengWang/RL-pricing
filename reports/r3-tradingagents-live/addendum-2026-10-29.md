## 4. Amendment: cost and cap (replaces §9's estimate)

The measured cost is $1.97 per session for three stocks. §9 estimated $0.52
at OpenAI prices. That comes to about $41 for a 21-session month and about
$124 for the window. At the pre-registered cap of $40 a month, the last
sessions of a long month would become cap holds. The cap is therefore:

- `LEDGER_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD` = **60**, up from 40;
- **$200** for the whole window, up from $120.

Both are within the budget approved on 2026-10-14 ($30–300 a month). A hold
caused by the cap still counts as a missed session and is reported.
