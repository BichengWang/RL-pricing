# R5 pre-registration addendum — 2026-11-02

Written before any R5 L2 result, while the L2 pipeline was prepared. It
records the implementation choices [`preregistration.md`](preregistration.md)
§5.2 left open, and the two places where the code's behaviour needs to be
stated before the first run. No claim, threshold, window, arm, strength or
alignment changes, and the preregistration is not edited.

## 1. Long-only projection after the sentiment multiplier

**Registered (§5.2 (i)).** The sentiment score multiplies the change of a
target weight, by `1 + s`, `1 + s/2`, `1 - s/2` or `1 - s` (matched to
opposed). The pre-registration does not say how the multiplied target meets
the engine's long-only budget.

**The L2 code.** Multiplies each asset's change by the registered factor,
clips every negative target at 0 (no short), and, if the targets sum above 1,
scales every asset down proportionally so that they sum to 1; the rest is
cash. The authors' code instead applies integer share trades in action order,
capping each sell at the shares held and each buy at available cash, and never
renormalises. The projection is therefore a **deviation** from the authors'
sequential cash/share caps and is listed under Deviations in the report; it
changes no arm, strength, alignment or claim.

## 2. Missing scores are per session

**Registered (§5.2 (ii)).** The two scores are appended to each asset's
features; a missing sentiment is 0 and a missing risk 3.

**The L2 code.** Applies the defaults per session: at a decision close, only
items published after the previous session's close and before this close are
used. A ticker-day with no such item takes 0/3 even if the ticker had a score
on an earlier day; scores are not carried forward. The same window is used
during `fit` and for checkpoint selection. This follows the authors'
environment, which reads the current day's row (a missing value becomes 0/3),
and the lagged alignment, which places a day's item after that day's close so
that it is the next session's score: the same-day alignment serves a day's
item at its own session, the lagged alignment at the next one.

**Placebo.** Each source row is mapped to the session at which it first
becomes visible under its alignment (the first session whose close is after
its publication), reduced to the latest row per (ticker, session), and the
per-session series is shifted circularly by the registered offset. The shifted
value is published one second before its new session's close. Non-session
(weekend) rows are therefore retained, as they are in the real arms, so the
placebo keeps each ticker's marginal distribution, missingness and
autocorrelation.

## 3. "Shipped defaults" means the reference entrants' parameters

**Registered (§5.2, Policies).** The lab's `ppo` and `cvar-ppo`, "each at its
shipped defaults at the commit used".

**The L2 arms.** Use the shipped reference entrants' parameters:
[`submissions/ppo`](../../submissions/ppo/submission.json) (PPO, 200,000
timesteps, 252-session episodes, a (64, 64) network, gamma 0.99, learning rate
3e-4) and [`submissions/cvar-ppo`](../../submissions/cvar-ppo/submission.json)
(the same PPO settings plus `cvar_alpha` 0.8 and `cvar_penalty` 1.0). These are
not the policy constructors' defaults (100,000 timesteps and `cvar_alpha`
0.9). The arms add only `llm_strength` (0.1, 0.01 or 0.001) and the score
source.

## 4. DeepSeek-V3's cutoff

No training cutoff is published in the DeepSeek-V3 model card or technical
report (§9). A third-party article states that its training data end in July
2024 (recorded as 2024-07-31), and is **[UNVERIFIED]**; the score arms'
`model_cutoffs` record that date with its source. The release date (2024-12-25)
is provenance, not a cutoff. Either date is after the 2019-2023 test window,
so every score arm stays `contamination: "possible"` and no LLM-feature claim
can earn "holds" without the label. Llama-3.3's documented "December 2023" is
recorded as 2023-12-31.

## 5. Baselines

`reproduce.py` produces the registered L2 comparison set for
the same test window and fee (10 bps):

- **Equal weight bought on the first fill and held**: the
  [`equal-weight-buy-hold`](../../submissions/equal-weight-buy-hold/submission.json)
  submission through `runner.evaluate` on `us-ndx80`;
- **the engine's monthly-rebalanced 1/N**: the
  [`equal-weight-monthly`](../../submissions/equal-weight-monthly/submission.json)
  submission the same way;
- **QQQ and `^NDX`**: daily closes from Yahoo Finance through
  `rl.marketdata.yahoodownloader.YahooDownloader`, adjusted forward for
  dividends from the task's 2013-01-01 start in the same way as the task's
  prices, aligned to the sessions from the first test decision to 2023-12-28,
  and written to `<out-root>/baselines/us-ndx80.json` with each series'
  SHA-256. QQQ is the total-return proxy and `^NDX` the price index; neither
  index series carries a fee.

No R5 result exists at the time of writing. The first L2 run is the one the
report will describe.
